## Primary listing: English

**App name**

Xinzhi ERP

**App card subtitle**

Run Shopify operations and customer support in one connected application.

**App introduction**

Connect Shopify to Xinzhi ERP for operations, fulfillment, and customer support.

**App details**

Requires prior account provisioning and approval by Xinzhi staff; no self-service signup. Installing does not grant system access. Connect Shopify operations and customer support: match ERP SKUs, import orders, publish inventory and merchant-managed shipments, and process eligible returns/refunds. Use storefront chat, assignment and tickets. Access is scoped by enterprise, role and store. Disputes are metadata-only; evidence stays in Shopify Admin.

**Features**

- Connect a Shopify store through Shopify authorization.
- Confirm the connected store and authorization status in Shopify App Home.
- Work in independently accessible ERP and customer-service workspaces.
- Match Shopify products and variants to ERP SKU records.
- Import orders and update eligible addresses, lines, discounts, or cancellation.
- Map locations and publish guarded inventory updates.
- Publish merchant-managed fulfillment and tracking details.
- Review customers and process eligible returns and refunds.
- Review dispute metadata; handle all evidence in Shopify Admin.
- Configure the Shopify support channel and storefront chat.
- Claim, reply to, transfer, and close conversations and manage related tickets.
- Revoke Shopify access when the merchant uninstalls the app.

**Search terms**

- order management
- product sync
- order sync
- cross-border ERP
- helpdesk

**Pricing**

Free. No recurring charge, one-time charge, usage charge, or off-platform app fee.

**Integrations**

Leave blank for the first listing unless a real, user-visible third-party integration is enabled in production. Do not list Shopify itself as an integration.

**Category and language declarations**

- Primary category/tag: `Orders and shipping` → `Inventory` → `ERP`.
- Secondary category/tag: `Store management` → `Support` → `Helpdesk`, because the complete customer-service workbench is part of the submitted application.
- Select only structured ERP and Helpdesk/Chat features that the exact review build and screencast demonstrate; do not select finance, marketing, draft-order, product-publishing, or unverified third-party-channel capabilities.
- App UI languages: claim `Simplified Chinese` only for the full product. Do not claim full English-language support; English is limited to the submitted reviewer path and Shopify connection preview.
- Primary listing language: English. Listing localization and full-product UI language support are separate declarations.

**Install eligibility**

- System access requires prior Xinzhi approval for every user and business. Shopify installation/OAuth does not create an ERP account or grant usage rights. Existing approved administrators link their store; unapproved users contact staff via the support page.
- Online Store sales channel required: Yes; the submitted customer-service workflow includes the `Xinzhi Chat` Theme App Extension. Merchants may choose whether to enable the embed on a theme.
- Shopify POS required: No.
- Store geography restriction: None unless production operations introduce a real restriction.
- Browser/desktop application required: No; the product is web-based.
- App type: regular app, not a Sales Channel.

## 不得写入 listing 的内容

- “最佳”、“100% 准确”、“提升销售额”等无证据保证。
- 未开放的商品写入/发布、历史全量订单、草稿订单、营销、收款对账、拒付附件上传或卡组织证据提交。
- Shopify 商标、价格、用户评论、输出成果保证或虚构客户数据。
- 精确送审版本中不存在、无法由审核账号访问或未在说明/录屏中真实证明的第三方渠道、自动化或其他客服能力。
